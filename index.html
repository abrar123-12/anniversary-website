📅 Our Journey

Two years, one beautiful story

It began with a dare in a message — this is our real story, day by day, exactly how it happened.

2 November 2024

It started with a dare

Out of nowhere, she messaged me: “I have a dare for you.” Just like that — one small message — our talking began, and my whole world quietly changed.

10–12 November 2024

The first storm

We fought. For three days, there was only silence between us — no messages, no talking. The quietest three days of my life.

14 November 2024

Talking again

And then, on the 14th, we started talking again — as if the silence itself had missed us. Some things are simply too strong to stay broken.

21 November 2024 · Night

She said it first 💗

That night she asked me, “Do you like anyone?” — and before the night ended, she told me the words herself: she loves me. The night everything became real.

December 2024

The first meet

We met on the street — our very first meet. But we could not say a single word to each other… her cousin was right there with her. So we just passed each other, hearts loud, mouths silent.

18 August 2025

Second meet

Months later, we met again. This time, the street felt a little more like ours.

24 September 2025

Bad luck 💔

I went to meet her without telling her — a surprise. But bad luck had other plans: her cousin was there, and the meet never happened. So close… and yet so far.

23 November 2025

Third meet

One more meeting, one more memory folded carefully into my heart.

3 December 2025

Fourth meet 🫣

A very, very special day. For the first time, we stayed together and talked for so, so long. Sometimes she would drift away into her own thoughts, lost somewhere far… and when it was time to leave, both our hearts felt so heavy. She cried in the car on the way back. 😓 Some days you never forget — this was one of them.

Today

Two years — and forever to go

From a dare in a message… to my wife. Happy anniversary, my soft-heart baby. This is not the end of the story. This is only chapter two. 💍
